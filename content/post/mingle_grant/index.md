I was awarded the HORIZON-CL4-2026-05-DIGITAL-EMERGING-02 grant (~$1,396,714.20) by Europe Horizon, MINGLE, to work on the safety of self-improving multi-agentic systems. I am the Principal Investigator from Oxford and part of a consortium of 30 excellent partners across academia and industry for a combined fund of ~$20 million. This is joint with co-PI Chris Summerfield and co-PI Philip Torr.
